## 全部文献

~~~dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID link(file.path, default(title, file.name)) AS "文献", year AS "年份", publication AS "期刊 / 会议", status AS "阅读状态"
FROM "10_Literature"
WHERE citekey
SORT year DESC
~~~
