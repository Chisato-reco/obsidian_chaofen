[[Dashboard|返回首页]] · [[90_Templates/模板索引|实验模板]]

只记录实际执行过的实验；尚未执行的方案放在项目任务中。

## 实验列表

~~~dataview
TABLE WITHOUT ID link(file.path, default(title, file.name)) AS "实验", date AS "日期", model_type AS "模型", status AS "状态", best_metric AS "结果"
FROM "30_Experiments"
WHERE contains(tags, "experiment")
SORT date DESC
~~~
